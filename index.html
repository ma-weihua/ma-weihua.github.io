About Me

I am a master's student at Nanjing University. My research interests lie at the intersection of political communication, digital platforms, and AI for Science.

My research examines how digital platforms shape public attention and political communication, with particular interests in the circulation of attention across media, political elites' communication, public opinion, and political polarization.

I am also interested in how artificial intelligence can advance social science research, particularly through developing and evaluating AI-assisted approaches to measuring complex social phenomena at scale.
